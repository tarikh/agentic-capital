# What venture firms actually do with AI

Tarikh Korula · November 2026 · A follow-up to [The rise of agentic capital](https://tarikh.me/writing/the-rise-of-agentic-capital)

When I wrote about agentic capital in March, I wondered what would happen when investors started building the system that picks the companies. Looking at how these firms work, I’d add a question. What does the system get to decide? At one firm it decides whose name goes on a recruiting list. At Rule30 it decides which companies get a check.

I’m a scout at ADIN, one of the funds below. This survey includes both predictive models and newer agents. Each row describes a particular job and links to the public account behind it.

| Job | Firm and system | What the AI does in practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find companies | **EQT Ventures · Motherbrain** | Flagged a rise in web traffic at AnyDesk. The partners investigated the product, sales and customer reviews, then led its Series A. [EQT’s deal account](https://stories.eqtventures.com/articles/on-eqt-ventures-investment-in-anydesk-%28revisited%29-best-in-class-remote-desktop-software) |
| Find companies | **Moonfire · sourcing engine** | Surfaced LiveFlow for the investment team. The people at Moonfire helped the founders hire a CTO. [Moonfire on LiveFlow](https://www.moonfire.com/stories/announcing-fund-ii/) |
| Find companies | **Earlybird · EagleEye** | Screens and ranks prospective investments. A company registration put Aleph Alpha on the team’s radar. Conversations with the founder gave the investors conviction. [How EagleEye works](https://earlybird.com/eagle-eye) · [Partner’s deal account](https://tech.eu/2023/11/21/what-europe-should-do-to-identify-tomorrows-billion-dollar-startups-today/) |
| Find companies | **InReach Ventures · DIG** | Agents draw on the firm’s past decisions and outcomes to score companies, prioritize new signals and draft outreach. Partners get a brief before the first call. [InReach on DIG](https://www.inreachventures.com/dig/) |
| Choose whom to interview | **Rebel Fund · Rebel Theorem** | Ranks each Y Combinator batch by the likelihood of a Series A markup. Partners still interview about 50 teams per batch to give themselves a chance of meeting an outlier further down the list. [Managing partner’s model notes](https://jaredheyman.medium.com/on-rebel-theorem-5-0-2c8270456e08) |
| Screen growth investments | **QuantumLight · Aleph** | Tracks venture-backed companies and analyzes their data to identify candidates the firm thinks could grow tenfold. That’s the model’s target. [QuantumLight on Aleph](https://quantumlightcapital.com/) |
| Check company performance | **Tribe Capital · Termina** | Tribe incubated a diligence platform that takes company operating data and benchmarks measures such as revenue growth, retention and customer concentration. [Tribe on Termina](https://tribecap.co/) · [Metrics the tool compares](https://www.termina.ai/) |
| Research investments | **Thrive Capital · Puck** | Answers the team’s research questions in chat and runs research tasks in the background using the firm’s data. [Head of AI on Puck](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/thesephist_agents-as-ordinary-software-principled-engineering-activity-7389341653901254656-Kh_w) |
| Prepare the investment committee | **B Capital · Bee Hive** | Drafts most committee memos, lays out the case for and against a deal, and can keep tracking a company after the firm passes. [Chief AI Officer interview](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/23/vc-job-chief-ai-officer) |
| Remember deals and passes | **Union Square Ventures · Arthur** | Keeps deal memos current, tracks what happens to companies the partners passed on, and answers questions in team email threads. Partners can correct it in the same thread. [USV’s build notes](https://www.usv.com/writing/meet-the-agents) |
| Win an allocation | **Alpaca VC · Gordon** | Mapped 50 people Autopoiesis Sciences might want to reach and a path to each. Showing the founders that list helped Alpaca secure a $1M allocation. [Founder and investor account](https://www.upstartsmedia.com/p/deep-dive-vc-firms-build-ai) |
| Help founders hire and sell | **SignalFire · Beacon** | Builds candidate and sales prospect lists for portfolio companies. SignalFire’s investors use the same platform to look for startups and prospective founders. [SignalFire on Beacon](https://www.signalfire.com/beacon-ai) |
| Help founders hire | **Basis Set · Pascal** | Produces a shortlist of candidates matching the skills and profile a founder wants to hire for. Basis Set calls Pascal an AI partner. Recruiting is one job it documents. [Basis Set’s founder FAQ](https://www.basisset.com/basis-set-faq) |
| Recommend an investment | **ADIN · AI investment committee** | Agents evaluated Infinity Artificial Intelligence Institute before ADIN invested $100,000. On another deal the agents favored, the humans met the founders and passed. [WIRED’s reporting](https://www.wired.com/story/ai-kill-venture-capital/) |
| Vote on an investment | **Vela Partners · machine GP** | Scores founders and casts one of three votes. A check requires two yeses. If the two human GPs split, the model decides the vote. [Vela’s voting rule](https://vela.partners/blog/what-is-a-quant-vc) |
| Select investments | **Rule30 · decision engine** | The model decides which startups get checks. People interview founders, seek allocations and execute deals. The firm reported 12 investments by December 2025. [Interview with co-founder Guy Conway](https://pathfounders.com/p/is-the-future-of-venture-capital-powered-by-ai) |

Two earlier examples put the current wave in perspective. In 2021, a Two Sigma Ventures partner estimated that its sourcing tool Georges had suggested about 12% of the firm’s investments, including NewtonX. That figure predates the spinout of Deviation Capital. At Hone Capital, investors tested a model against a 2015 seed cohort and checked which companies reached Series A within 15 months. Both firms could ask whether software had spotted something their investors might have missed. [Two Sigma Ventures on Georges](https://www.businessinsider.com/two-sigma-ventures-uses-an-ai-tool-named-georges-to-source-deals-2021-5) · [Hone’s retrospective](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/technology-media-and-telecommunications/our-insights/a-machine-learning-approach-to-venture-capital)

One distinction matters when reading these accounts. Alpaca can point to a founder who made room in a round. USV can go back to a company it passed on and check what happened. Vela can tell you exactly when the machine’s vote counts. The question I’d love to see a firm answer next is just as specific. Which company did the partners want to pass on, what did the model see, and what happened to the check they wrote because of it?
